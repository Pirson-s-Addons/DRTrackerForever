## What it does

Every crowd control of the same type in a row lasts less: the first one in full, the second half,
the third a quarter, and after that the enemy is immune, until 18 seconds after the last one ends.
**DR Tracker Forever** shows it as icons above your target, focus and player frames: the border and
the text say how long the **next** one lasts (50%, 25%, 0%) and the spiral shows when it resets.

WoW Forever hides the combat log from addons and its own DR system is switched off, so the DR is
counted from **your casts and your group's**, the misses and immunities the game reports, and the
effects the game lets addons see (all of them out of combat).

## Commands

- `/drf` — settings
- `/drf unlock` / `/drf lock` — move the icons
- `/drf test` — sample icons for 20 seconds
- `/drf clear` — forget every tracked DR
